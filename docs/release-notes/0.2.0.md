## Hubble 0.2.0

Hubble is now a desktop app. The website is where you download it; your projects live in the app on your computer.

- Hubble for Windows, macOS and Linux.
- Connect Hubble for Chrome once: it then saves AI conversations and tabs straight into the project you choose, and brings what you saved into your next AI chat.
- If Hubble isn't open, the extension keeps what you saved and delivers it when Hubble starts.
- Hubble reads the tabs open in Chrome itself (save a session, organize tabs, import from history) and reopens a saved session in a Chrome window — no website page in between.
- Context detection (optional, off until you choose) now works in the app: Hubble can suggest which project the pages and chats you use belong to.
- Your work is kept safe if Hubble or your computer stops unexpectedly.
- Hubble proves itself to Hubble for Chrome before the extension shares anything, so another program on your computer can't pose as Hubble.
- Sign in from the app (through your browser) to sync projects and use AI connectors. Everything else works without an account.
- Used Hubble on the web? Your projects are still in that browser. hubblehq.vercel.app/move saves them to a file you import in Settings → Projects — projects made at the old hubble-hq.vercel.app address can be saved this way once that address is back online.

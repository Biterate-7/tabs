## Hubble 0.2.0

Hubble is now a desktop app. The website is where you download it; your projects live in the app on your computer.

- Hubble for Windows, macOS and Linux.
- Connect Hubble for Chrome once: it then saves AI conversations and tabs straight into the project you choose, and brings what you saved into your next AI chat.
- If Hubble isn't open, the extension keeps what you saved and delivers it when Hubble starts.
- Sign in from the app (through your browser) to sync projects and use AI connectors.
- Used Hubble on the web? Your projects are still in that browser. hubblehq.vercel.app/move saves them to a file you import in Settings → Projects — projects made at the old hubble-hq.vercel.app address can be saved this way once that address is back online.

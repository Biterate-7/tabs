Hubble for Chrome

Let Hubble use this AI app?

To put what you chose in Hubble into a new chat, Hubble for Chrome needs access to this site.

  • Used only when you click Use in AI in Hubble.
  • It opens a new chat, types your message and attaches your files there.
  • It presses send only when you chose Send automatically.
  • It doesn't read your other chats. You can remove access in Chrome's extension settings.
